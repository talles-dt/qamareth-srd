# Influência Política — Bastions & IP

## Rebel Faction Influence Points (IP)

IP measures underground territory control and **moral high ground**. Earned through acts against the Empire.

|| Task | IP Gain | Faction |
||------|--------|--------|
|| Sabotage Imperial harvest | +1 | Silent Scythe |
|| Preserve grove from logging | +2 | Green Shield |
|| Smuggle goods past patrol | +1 per 100kg | Ashen Path |
|| Deliver contraband cargo | +2 per trip | Deepend Smugglers |
|| Win military skirmish | +3 | Ember Vanguard |
|| Host Silver Dawn liturgy | +4 at Dawnspire beacon (298 IE) | Silver Dawn |

> **Note**: Each +10 IP within a single region triggers **bastion upgrade** (costs follow `17-bastions.md`).
> Imperial faction IP accrues as **Standing**, not rebel IP.
